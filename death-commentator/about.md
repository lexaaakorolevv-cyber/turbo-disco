# Death Commentator

Choose how the game reacts to a fail:

- **Kind**: encouragement and support.
- **Neutral**: calm observations.
- **Roast**: harsh insults and profanity aimed at your gameplay.

Open **Comments** from the main menu or pause screen to choose a tone. Press
**На экран** to demonstrate a large banner on the actual game screen. The banner
uses Geode's viewport overlay and survives ordinary retries. There are 69
Russian and 69 English phrases, with a bundled Cyrillic font.

Version 1.1 enables the requested Roast preset on its first launch: center
screen, every eligible death, no minimum gap, 3.5-second duration. Subsequent
changes are saved locally. Kind and Neutral modes remain available.

Windows x64 and Android64, Geometry Dash 2.2081, Geode 5.11.1.
